# Brainstorm

Use existing context, then run a tight Socratic conversation: ask one
high-leverage question at a time, or a small tightly related set, to surface
purpose, constraints, success criteria, and the smallest useful scope.
Once clear, present 2–3 viable directions with trade-offs, lead with a
recommendation, and converge with the user.
Complete with the chosen direction, explicit exclusions, and remaining risks;
leave planning and implementation for the next request.
