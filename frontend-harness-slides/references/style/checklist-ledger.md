# Checklist Ledger

## Aesthetic Thesis
A plain, trustworthy ledger — the tone of a careful financial or administrative
record — focused entirely on completeness and readiness checks. The point is
diligence: every item accounted for, nothing skipped.

## Emotional Register & Fit
Trustworthy, practical, and restrained — the register of a careful audit. Reach
for it on readiness checks, acceptance criteria, and final quality checklists,
where conveying that everything was verified is the message. As a reading-first
style it carries real detail, but it fights expressive storytelling; the plain
ledger reassures in a sign-off and bores in a keynote.

## Color Temperament
Keep a very light, clean ground with a faint ledger-line feel, and use a calm
confirming green for checks against deep, steady text. The palette is quiet and
accounting-like. Why: the trust of a ledger comes from plainness and a clear
"done" mark; loud or varied color would make a careful record look casual.

## Typographic Voice
Favor a clean, traditional character — a plain sans or a light serif — that reads
as steady and orderly, with consistent alignment down the list. Keep it modest and
even. Why: the credibility of a checklist rests on calm, uniform legibility; an
expressive voice would undercut the sober, accounted-for feel.

## Spatial Temperament
It wants a ledger sensibility: a formal header and a clean list of strict items,
each with a clear check state, aligned in orderly rows. Whether it is a single
readiness ledger or a two-column audit checklist is free; the invariant is a
uniform, aligned, checkable list that reads as complete and accounted-for.

## Material & Atmosphere
The surface feels like ledger paper: light, faintly ruled, orderly, and quiet.
Depth is nearly flat — order comes from alignment and rules, not shadow. The
atmosphere is calm, diligent, and dependable.

## Signature Gestures
The recurring identity marks are ledger vocabulary: a formal header, strict
checklist items, clear checkmarks, and steady aligned rows. Use them to carry real
checks — real criteria, real readiness. The uniform checkable-row grammar is the
signature; decorative or irregular layout breaks it.

## Motion & Interaction Register
Movement is checking off: items confirm one by one with a check, a total or
readiness state resolves, an item reveals why it matters. Interaction can check an
item or expand its rationale. Keep motion calm and cumulative. Why: the animation
mirrors completing a checklist, so lively motion would contradict the sober
diligence.

## Identity Invariants vs Freedoms
Keep constant to stay this style: a very light ledger-ruled ground with a calm
confirming green, a plain steady traditional voice, a uniform aligned checkable
list that reads as complete, flat ledger depth, and calm cumulative motion. Free
to vary on every slide: single ledger versus two-column checklist, which items
appear, and how checks and rationale are revealed.

## What Breaks the Spell
It dies when it gets showy: a dark or vivid background, varied decorative color,
an expressive typeface, an irregular non-list layout, or flashy motion. If the
moment calls for energy, emotion, or a single big claim, the ledger is too plain —
use a keynote or punchline style instead.
