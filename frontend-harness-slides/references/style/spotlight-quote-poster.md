# Spotlight Quote Poster

## Aesthetic Thesis
A darkened stage where a single spotlight falls on a few powerful words, creating
a held, reflective pause so a statement can land and linger. The emptiness and the
light do the work; the words are meant to be sat with.

## Emotional Register & Fit
Reflective, dramatic, and a little reverent — the register of a theatrical pause.
Reach for it on closing statements, guiding philosophies, mission lines, and
deliberate breaths between sections, where you want the audience to stop and
absorb one sentence. It fights procedural, explanatory, or data content; there is
nothing to walk through here, only something to feel, so keep it away from steps
and evidence.

## Color Temperament
Sink the surface into deep darkness — a near-black charcoal — and let a single
soft pool of light lift the words out of the shadow. Text is luminous and light; a
single muted, elegant accent can mark an attribution or a key word, never more.
Why: the contrast between vast darkness and one lit statement is the entire
effect; extra color or an evenly bright field dissolves the spotlight and the
gravity with it.

## Typographic Voice
Favor a large, elegant, expressive character with literary warmth — often a
graceful high-contrast serif, frequently italic — that reads as considered and
human, so the quote feels weighty and sincere. Any attribution stays small,
quiet, and spaced out beneath the statement. Why: the words are the performance,
so they need a voice with real presence and emotion; a plain or utilitarian
typeface makes a profound line feel like a caption.

## Spatial Temperament
It wants one statement held in a large dark field, lit and centered in attention,
with generous emptiness around it so nothing competes. The quote can sit centered
in the pool of light or be anchored to one side while the rest melts into shadow.
Where exactly the light falls and how the words are set are free; the invariant is
a single luminous statement surrounded by dominant darkness.

## Material & Atmosphere
The surface is deep, matte shadow with a soft radial fall of light — smooth and
gradientless except for that gentle glow — so it feels like a stage rather than a
screen. Depth is pure light and dark: the words emerge from shadow, everything
else recedes. The atmosphere is hushed, still, and emotionally charged.

## Signature Gestures
The recurring identity marks are minimal and theatrical: the pool of spotlight,
the quotation itself as hero, and a small, understated attribution set apart. Use
light as the only real ornament. Any added decoration betrays the restraint the
style depends on.

## Motion & Interaction Register
Movement is slow and atmospheric: the light swells gently, the words fade up and
settle, and if attention shifts, the spotlight drifts rather than cuts.
Interaction, if any, is barely there — a soft emphasis, a gentle reveal of the
attribution. Keep everything unhurried. Why: the style is engineering a pause, so
any quick or energetic motion breaks the stillness it exists to create.

## Identity Invariants vs Freedoms
Keep constant to stay this style: a deep dark field, a single soft pool of light,
luminous text with at most one muted accent, a large expressive literary type
voice, one statement held in dominant emptiness, and slow atmospheric motion.
Free to vary on every slide: the exact words, whether the light is centered or off
to one side, the specific accent, and how gently the statement is revealed.

## What Breaks the Spell
It dies the moment it fills up or brightens: multiple points on screen, a light or
busy background, several colors, a plain utilitarian typeface, hard even lighting,
or quick snappy motion. It also dies if asked to explain — lists, steps, or data
have no place under the spotlight; move that content to a reading-first or process
style and keep this for the pause.
