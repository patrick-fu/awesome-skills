# Delegation

Before interrupting or replacing a delegate, inspect its status, processes,
dirty work and generated artifacts; preserve recoverable results. Reuse context
that still serves the target. A plan-only return with useful groundwork gets
one narrower outcome and explicit exit condition before replacement.

## Boundaries

- **Dependency:** consume a required result before taking an action that depends
  on it, including contract-changing review before repair.
- **Completion:** required results must be terminal, inspected and incorporated
  into acceptance. A delegate's success claim alone is insufficient.
- **Handoff:** pending work may cross a turn or handoff boundary only after
  confirming the receiver, task identity, state, artifacts, remaining work and
  recovery method. Verify receiver access and continuation from runtime evidence
  or a receiver-side probe. Preserve single write ownership and record that the
  task is pending. A saved handle alone does not prove recoverability.

If handles or results may disappear across the boundary, collect and consume
the necessary results first. If execution is genuinely blocked, save recoverable
artifacts and safely suspend or retire transient work with an explicit remaining
outcome. Do not assume persistence or replace a reviewer solely for silence.
